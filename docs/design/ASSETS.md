# Recursos visuales · 0.1.1

Generados con la herramienta integrada de imágenes de Codex. La foto facilitada por el usuario se usó como referencia del coche; no se incluye en el repositorio. Las imágenes finales se incluyen como WebP optimizado con transparencia real y funcionan sin conexión.

- `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/polo_hero.webp`: imagen de estudio del Polo rojo, usada en Inicio y bienvenida con ajuste proporcional, sin recorte.
- `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/polo_icon.webp`: cochecito rojo simple de perfil. El icono adaptativo deja un margen del 18 % para las máscaras de Android, sobre fondo grafito. Sustituye al icono anterior; se descartó la propuesta de monograma P a petición del usuario.

## Prompt de la imagen principal

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: production hero image in a premium Android car care app, landscape 1536x864 composition. Input image is a subject reference only: the owner's Volkswagen Polo Mk5 6R 2013 red five-door hatchback. Create a fresh, exquisitely realistic automotive studio rendering of precisely this model, factory proportions, stock silver multispoke wheels, stock pre-facelift headlights, five doors, glossy solid red paint. Full car viewed front-left three-quarter angle, front pointing left, similar to reference but a little lower and wider catalog angle. All four corners and both visible tires fully in frame. The car should occupy 90% of image width and 80% of height with compact margins. Meticulous coherent perspective, authentic short hatchback proportions, clean door seams, beautifully rendered detailed glass headlights and realistic tires. Transparent background with genuine alpha, only a soft restrained contact shadow immediately beneath wheels. No backdrop, no floor rectangle, no text, no watermark, no UI. Neutral white large softbox highlights, beautiful red paint, dark glass with subtle interior detail. Avoid stylized cartoon, low-poly, vector look, toy proportions, dramatic neon, added spoilers or modern Golf body. Reference photo exhibition setting must not appear.

## Prompt del icono final

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: Android launcher foreground for Polo App. Exactly one simple little red five-door hatchback car, inspired by a 2013 Volkswagen Polo, viewed directly from the side and pointing left. Friendly, clean, contemporary flat vector-like illustration, professionally designed icon with confident smooth curves and accurate compact hatchback proportions. Single solid rich red body, subtly darker red lower edge, two simple dark graphite circular wheels with small light grey hubs, one unified dark blue-grey window area divided by a slim red B pillar, tiny warm-white front headlight. Slightly rounded geometric shapes. At most 6 colors. No gradients, no chrome, no photorealism, no 3D, no road, no shadow, no lettering, no badges, no VW mark, no circle, no tile or background. Genuine transparent alpha background. Square image, car horizontally centered and vertically centered, occupies 80% of width and around 40% of height, ample empty transparent margins. Entire vehicle visible and balanced. Make it immediately legible as a small RED CAR at 32 pixels. This is an understated charming car icon, not a detailed illustration.
